A little field guide

Choose for your corner, not just the photo.

A plant is happiest when its new spot suits it. These three small checks make choosing feel simple.

01 / LIGHT

Notice your window

Bright indirect light works for many houseplants. Think about where the sun lands during the day.

02 / SPACE

Measure the spot

A shelf, tabletop or open floor corner can each suit a different shape and size of plant.

03 / ROUTINE

Keep care realistic

Pick something that fits the time and attention you can comfortably give it.
