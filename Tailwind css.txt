what is CDN?
    CDN stands for Content Delivery Network. 
    in simple terms, a CDN is a network of distributed servers strategically placed around the world to deliver web Content
    (like images, stylesheets, scripts and videos) more efficiently to users.
    ex:<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@tailwindcss/browser@4"></script>
